Each video, day by day

Each date column shows the views that video had at the last read of that day, and in green what that day added. Blank means not posted yet. A dash means the shop was not read that day.

What the numbers mean
Views
The count shown on the TikTok video itself, paid and unpaid together.
New
Views added since the last read on an earlier day.
Orders and sales
Sales of that video alone through GMV Max ads, every campaign added together. Not the whole campaign, and not sales that came without ads.
GMV Max ads
Whether the video is in a GMV Max campaign. "In ads, not shown yet" means it is in the campaign but TikTok has not shown it as an ad. "NOT in ads" means it is in no campaign. "Ads not read" means the shop's ads page could not be read since the video was posted, so it is not known yet.
Which videos
Only the videos saved to Downloads and posted by phone on each posting day.
